pathology, and reasoning annotations. Preserve dataset attribution when using
or redistributing it.

The archive passed a full ZIP CRC check after upload. The extracted data contain
47,273 files (approximately 6.9 GB), including 11,439 BUS-Expert records and
5,163 BUS-Lesion records.
